Signs You Need This

Don't Wait Too Long

Dark Smoke or Strong Odor

If your fireplace smokes into the room or smells strongly, the flue may be blocked or lined with heavy creosote.

It's Been Over a Year

The NFPA recommends annual chimney inspections — regardless of how much you use it.

You Recently Moved In

You don't know when it was last cleaned. An inspection before first use is the safe move.
