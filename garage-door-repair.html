Signs You Need This

Don't Wait Too Long

Loud Grinding or Banging

Unusual noise usually means worn rollers or a spring starting to fail.

Door Won't Open or Close

Usually a broken spring — the biggest safety hazard on this list. Stop using it and call.

Door Looks Crooked

A door hanging unevenly signals cable or track misalignment.
